How Weather Affects Safety

  • Night-time routes slightly lower the safety score.
  • Heavy rain or thunderstorms may also reduce safety.
  • These adjustments are applied on top of your crime-based scores.

Dataset Columns Used

The live safety score is influenced by these columns:

  • Street name
  • Nearest metro station distance (km)
  • Type of road
  • Crime (total index)
  • Nearest police chowki (km)
  • Population density
  • Rape
  • Kidnapping & Abduction_Total
  • Acid attack
  • Assault on Women
  • Sexual Harassment
  • Use of criminal force to women
  • Stalking
  • Other Assault on Women
  • District
  • Safety (label/score)
